What we do

The last gesture before your customer

A cream can be perfectly formulated, an oil can come from the finest grove in the Valdelsa: the first thing a buyer actually touches is still the box. For more than twenty years Chianti Servizi has taken care of exactly that moment, packing on behalf of cosmetics brands and artisan food producers who would rather leave nothing to chance.

Our method

Skilled hands, before machines

Our jobs range from a few hundred units for a limited-edition gift box to large seasonal runs. That is why we combine semi-automatic lines with hand packing, choosing case by case whatever protects the product better and does more justice to the brand.

  • A prototype before production. We build real samples for approval, not renderings.
  • A dedicated team per job. The same people from the first unit to the last.
  • Full traceability. Batch, quantity and checks recorded on every pallet.
  • Materials on request. We can source cartons, ribbons and components for you.

How we work

From the first conversation to a finished pallet

Stage 01

Analysis

We study the product, the materials and the volumes. If useful, we come and see your current line.

Stage 02

Prototype

We build physical samples, set cycle times and quote per unit, with no surprises later.

Stage 03

Production

We set up the station, train the team and run the batch with documented sample checks.

Stage 04

Delivery

Outer packing, logistics labelling and shipment to your warehouse or straight to the stores.
