What we do

Contract packaging, built around your product

Two separate departments, cosmetics and food, and a set of operations that combine differently for every job: from a single carton to a complete gift box, ready for the shelf.

Sector 01

Cosmetics and perfumery

We work on finished, sealed products: creams, serums, fragrances, make-up, beauty supplements. Our job begins where your filling line ends.

  • Cartoning by hand and semi-automatic, including leaflets and dosing accessories.
  • Cellophane wrapping of single cartons and gift boxes, in clear or soft-touch film.
  • Gift boxes and sets: bespoke die-cuts, inner trays, hand-applied ribbons and seals.
  • Samples and miniatures for perfumeries, magazines and promotional corners.
  • Seals and serialisation, including tamper-evident bands.
  • Reworking of batches whose packaging must be updated or adapted to another market.

Sector 02

Artisan food

We only handle products that are already packed and sealed at source: we do not process unwrapped food. What we take care of is the outer layer that turns it into a gift or a shelf product.

  • Festive baked goods: colombe, panettoni and pandori, hand-wrapped or heat-sealed, with ribbon and tag.
  • Hampers and Christmas boxes: composition, weight balancing, clear film and ribbon finish.
  • Oil, wine, honey and preserves: single cartons, twin packs, cases and wooden crates.
  • Chocolate and pralines: wrapping, tray insertion, seasonal boxes.
  • Corporate boxes for gifts and year-end hampers, personalised with the client’s brand.
  • Labelling with batch, expiry date and the mandatory information for each destination market.

Operations

Everything we can do for you

These operations combine freely: many clients start with a single one and end up entrusting us with the whole cycle.

Cartoning

Carton erecting, product and accessory insertion, closing and sealing.

Cellophane wrapping

Protective film on single units or multipacks, with clean folds and an easy-open tab.

Kits and promotional sets

Assembly of bundles, giveaways, seasonal gift boxes and multipacks.

Gift packaging

Hand wrapping, satin ribbons, wax seals, tags and personalised cards.

Labelling and coding

Adhesive labels, seals, barcodes, batch numbers, dates and serial codes.

Quality control

Visual inspection, sorting, screening of non-conforming batches and recovery of sound units.

Outer packing and logistics

Shipping cases, palletising, logistics labels and e-commerce order preparation.

Material storage

We hold your cartons, ribbons and components until production starts.

Prototyping

Real samples, resistance tests and cycle-time estimates before any job is committed.
