Our story

A workshop, not an assembly line

We are based in Poggibonsi, halfway between Florence and Siena. We have been packing other people’s products for over twenty years, with the care we would want for our own.

The beginning

Born where the products are made

Chianti Servizi started in the late nineties in the Valdelsa, a valley where small food producers live side by side with a cosmetics supply chain that serves some of the best known brands in the world. On one side olive mills, wineries and pastry workshops; on the other, chemical plants and component makers for the perfume industry.

What was missing in between was someone who could do the last step properly: take a finished product and turn it into something you can put in a window, ship, or give as a present. We began with a handful of people and a small unit, assembling Christmas hampers for local producers.

Today we work with Italian and international brands, but the rule has not changed: we only take on jobs we are confident we can finish well.

Milestones

Twenty-five years, in short

1998

The first Christmas

The company starts out assembling hampers and festive boxes for farm producers around the Valdelsa.

2005

Into cosmetics

First cartoning and cellophane contracts for the Tuscan perfumery supply chain. Working areas are separated.

2012

A new facility

We move to the current site, with a dedicated warehouse for client materials and semi-automatic lines.

2019

Kits and e-commerce

As online sales grow, so do promotional sets, sampling and ship-ready packing.

Today

Two sectors, one team

Cosmetics and food live under the same roof, in separate departments, with staff trained on both.

How we operate

Four things we will not trade

Saying it straight

If a deadline is not realistic we say so beforehand, not mid-batch. We would rather lose an order than your trust.

The finish matters

A crooked ribbon or an imprecise fold undoes the work of whoever created the product. Where it counts, we check unit by unit.

People stay

Many of our operators have been here for more than ten years: they know our clients’ products by heart.

As little waste as possible

We optimise client materials, recover reusable offcuts and favour certified paper and board.

The area

Why here

Poggibonsi is a convenient junction: we sit on the Florence–Siena road, forty minutes from Florence airport and two hours from the Tyrrhenian ports. For an overseas client that means watching your batch being packed in the morning and having dinner in Florence.

More importantly, we are inside the district: nearly all our suppliers of cartons, ribbons and components are within fifty kilometres. When a material runs short, we often go and collect it ourselves.

  • Florence 45 km — Poggibonsi Nord exit on the dual carriageway
  • Siena 30 km
  • Certaldo and the Florentine Valdelsa 15 minutes away
